Privacy Policy

Effective 31 August 2026

Campus Inbox is an independent student productivity project operated by Aditya Pratap Singh. It organizes college Gmail messages into understandable priority categories while collecting as little information as practical.

1. Information we access

When you choose to connect Gmail, Google OAuth grants Campus Inbox read-only access to your Gmail account. The app may access the sender, subject, date, message identifier and message text of up to the 75 most recent messages received during the previous 30 days. It also obtains your Gmail address once to create a private owner identifier.

Campus Inbox cannot send, edit or delete your Gmail messages. It never receives your Google password or OTP.

2. How information is used

Gmail information is used only to provide the visible features of Campus Inbox:

  • identify exams, assignments, attendance notices and deadlines;
  • surface hackathons, internships, scholarships and useful opportunities;
  • classify routine announcements as lower priority;
  • display the sender, subject, preview, date, priority and classification reason; and
  • avoid downloading unchanged messages repeatedly.

Gmail information is not used for advertising, credit decisions, surveillance or training general-purpose artificial-intelligence models.

3. Information stored

The cache stores the Gmail message ID, sender, subject, a preview limited to 240 characters, received date, priority and classification reason. Complete email bodies are processed for classification and then discarded. Your Gmail address is converted into a keyed private owner ID and is not stored in the cache.

OAuth tokens are encrypted using authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption. The browser receives only a signed, HTTP-only session cookie—not the token itself.

4. Retention and deletion

Campus Inbox keeps only the latest cached set used by the dashboard. As new messages enter the newest-75 window, older cached records leave it. Disconnecting revokes Google access and ends the local session. Selecting Delete my data removes all cached records associated with your private owner ID, revokes Google access and ends the session.

Deleting Campus Inbox data never deletes the original messages in Gmail.

5. Sharing and human access

Campus Inbox does not sell Google user data. It does not share Gmail content with advertisers, data brokers or unrelated third parties. Data may be processed by the hosting and database providers required to operate the service, under appropriate security controls, after public deployment.

Humans do not read Gmail content except when you provide explicit permission for specific support, when necessary to investigate security abuse, or when legally required.

6. Google API Limited Use

Campus Inbox’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Access is limited to the user-facing email-prioritization features described in this policy.

7. Security

Protections include encrypted OAuth tokens, isolated user ownership, signed HTTP-only cookies, CSRF protection, rate limits, security headers and restricted database queries. No system can guarantee absolute security; suspected issues should be reported promptly.

8. Your choices

  • Do not connect Gmail if you do not want the app to access messages.
  • Disconnect Gmail at any time from the dashboard.
  • Use Delete my data to remove cached records.
  • Revoke Campus Inbox directly from your Google Account permissions.

9. Changes

This policy may change as Campus Inbox develops. Material changes to Google-data use will be disclosed before the new use begins. The effective date above identifies the current version.

10. Contact

Developer: Aditya Pratap Singh. Until a dedicated public support address is created, privacy questions may be sent to the developer-contact address displayed by Google during OAuth consent. A public support address will be added before public launch.
